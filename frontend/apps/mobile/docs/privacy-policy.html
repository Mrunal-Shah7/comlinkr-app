This Privacy Policy applies to the ComLinkr mobile application (available on Android and iOS), our website at www.comlinkr.com, and all related services (collectively, the "Service"). By using ComLinkr, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information We Collect

Account Information

When you create an account, we collect your name, email address, city, and optional profile details such as your bio, interests, and profile photo.

Usage Data

We automatically collect information about how you interact with ComLinkr, including pages viewed, features used, time spent, and general device information (device type, OS version, app version).

Location Data

We use your selected city to show relevant community content. We do not track your precise GPS location unless you explicitly grant location permissions for features like nearby events.

Content You Share

Posts, comments, messages, listings, and any other content you create or share on ComLinkr is stored to provide the Service.

Camera & Photos

If you choose to upload a profile photo or post images, we request access to your device's camera or photo library. This access is only used when you actively choose to upload content.

2. How We Use Your Information

Provide & Improve Services

We use your information to operate ComLinkr, personalise your feed, suggest relevant events and listings, and improve our features based on usage patterns.

Communications

We may send push notifications and emails about activity on your posts, messages, and events you're interested in. You can manage notification preferences in Settings. We use Resend (Privacy Policy) to deliver transactional emails such as password resets and account notifications.

Safety & Security

We use information to detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and violations of our community guidelines. This includes automated content moderation.

Analytics

We use Firebase Analytics (Google) to understand aggregate usage patterns and improve the app experience. Firebase Analytics collects anonymised usage data. For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy.

3. How We Share Your Information

Community Visibility

Your profile, posts, and listings are visible to other ComLinkr members in your community. Direct messages are only visible to conversation participants.

Service Providers

We work with trusted third-party services for hosting, analytics, and notifications. These providers are bound by confidentiality agreements and cannot use your data for their own purposes.

Legal Requirements

We may disclose information if required by law, legal process, or to protect the safety and rights of ComLinkr, our users, or the public.

No Selling of Data

We do not sell your personal information to advertisers or any third parties. Your data is used solely to power and improve the ComLinkr experience.

4. Data Security

Encryption

All data transmitted between your device and our servers is encrypted using industry-standard TLS/SSL protocols. Sensitive data is encrypted at rest.

Access Controls

We implement strict access controls and authentication measures. Only authorised personnel can access user data, and all access is logged and monitored.

Regular Audits

We conduct regular security audits to identify and address vulnerabilities in our systems.

5. Your Rights & Choices

Access & Download

You can view and download your personal data at any time from Settings → Privacy → Download My Data.

Edit & Update

You can update your profile information, interests, and preferences at any time through your profile settings.

Delete Account

You can permanently delete your account and all associated data from Settings → Account → Delete Account. This action is irreversible.

Opt Out

You can opt out of non-essential communications and personalised content from Settings → Privacy.

6. Cookies & Tracking

Local Storage

ComLinkr uses local storage on your device to save preferences, authentication tokens, and cached content for a faster experience.

Analytics

We use Firebase Analytics to understand aggregate usage patterns. Individual users are not personally tracked across other apps or websites.

7. Children's Privacy

Age Requirement

ComLinkr is intended for users aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we discover such data has been collected, we will delete it promptly. If you believe we have inadvertently collected information from a child under 13, please contact us at support@comlinkr.com.

9. CCPA & CPRA – Rights for California Users

If you are a California resident, you have the following rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA):

Right to Know

You have the right to request information about the categories and specific pieces of personal information we have collected about you, the sources, the business purpose, and the third parties with whom we share it.

Right to Delete

You have the right to request that we delete personal information we have collected from you, subject to certain exceptions.

Right to Opt-Out of Sale

We do not sell personal information. You do not need to opt out.

Right to Non-Discrimination

We will not discriminate against you for exercising any of your CCPA rights.

Right to Correct

You have the right to request correction of inaccurate personal information we maintain about you.

To submit a CCPA request, contact us at support@comlinkr.com.

10. CalOPPA – California Online Privacy Protection Act

In accordance with CalOPPA, we agree to the following: users can visit our app and website anonymously; this Privacy Policy link is clearly accessible; our Privacy Policy link includes the word "Privacy" and is easily findable; users will be notified of any Privacy Policy changes on this page; users are able to change their personal information by logging into their account or contacting us.

Do Not Track: We do not respond to Do Not Track (DNT) browser signals as there is no industry-standard approach to DNT compliance at this time.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes through the app or via email. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will always reflect the most recent revision. Continued use of ComLinkr after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
