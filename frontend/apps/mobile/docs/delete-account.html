Delete Your Account

We're sorry to see you go. Here's everything you need to know about deleting your ComLinkr account.

⚠️ Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Before proceeding, please note:

  • All your posts, listings, and community content will be permanently deleted
  • Your profile, messages, and account data will be removed
  • Any active property agreements or applications will be cancelled
  • You will lose access to all your saved items and history

Method 1 — Delete from the App (Recommended)

  • 1

    Open ComLinkr

    Launch the ComLinkr app on your Android or iOS device and make sure you are logged in.

  • 2

    Go to Settings

    Tap the hamburger menu (☰) in the top-left corner, then tap Profile → Settings.

  • 3

    Find "Delete Account"

    Scroll to the bottom of the Settings screen. You will see a red "Delete account" button.

  • 4

    Confirm Deletion

    Tap Delete account → tap Continue on the confirmation prompt. Your account will be scheduled for deletion and you will be signed out immediately.

  • 5

    Deletion Complete

    Your account and all associated data will be permanently deleted within 30 days of your request.

Method 2 — Request via Email

If you cannot access the app, you can request account deletion by emailing us directly.

  • 1

    Send an email to support@comlinkr.com

    Use the subject line: "Delete My Account Request"

  • 2

    Include your account details

    Include your registered email address and full name so we can locate your account.

  • 3

    We will confirm within 48 hours

    Our support team will confirm your deletion request by email within 48 hours.

  • 4

    Account deleted within 30 days

    Your account and all data will be permanently deleted within 30 days of confirmation.

What Data Gets Deleted vs Kept

Data Type Action Retention Period
Profile (name, email, photo, bio) Deleted Within 30 days
Posts, comments, listings Deleted Within 30 days
Direct messages Deleted Within 30 days
Saved items and preferences Deleted Within 30 days
Transaction/legal records (if any) Kept As required by law (up to 7 years)
Anonymised analytics data Kept Retained in anonymised form
