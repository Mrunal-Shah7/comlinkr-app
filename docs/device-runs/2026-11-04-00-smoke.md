## What went wrong while driving the phone

Recorded so the next run avoids it; none of these is an app bug.

- The MCP server misread the wireless serial `adb-d9bf1d37-VVLKjS (2)._adb-tls-connect._tcp` (it has a space), and
  reported "no authorized device". Fixed by `adb connect 192.168.137.152:41833` and passing that serial.
- Screenshots from the scrcpy session lagged a step behind the screen. Stopping the session and using
  `adb screencap` gave current frames.
- Taps sent while a menu or dialog was still animating did nothing (the first Log Out confirm, a tab tap during the
  toast's close).
- The keyboard covered the password field; the next tap hit the keyboard's settings key and opened Gboard
  settings. Closed with Back, nothing changed there. Fields are now found and focused through the UI tree.
- In the signed-in menu the Dark Mode switch sits under the pinned Log Out footer; one tap opened the Log Out
  dialog and was cancelled.
- `adb shell screenrecord` is refused on this phone (Permission denied, even in `/data/local/tmp`); scrcpy's
  `--record` works.
