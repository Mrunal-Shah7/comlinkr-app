## What went wrong while driving the phone

- After a Fast Refresh closed an open article, one Back left Expo Go for the home screen and the next tap opened
  the **Play Store (Google Play Books)**. Nothing was changed there; Back returned to the launcher. A second time,
  a double Back after opening a composer left the app again (no taps followed).
- Since then every step runs `app.sh` first (reopens Expo Go if anything else is in front) and sends one Back at a
  time, checking the focused window after each.
- A swipe that started on a compose-sheet row opened "Create event" (a press, not a scroll); nothing was posted.
